YTD figures (Jan–Sep) are from the September statements dated 19 Oct 2026; January–April statements added 22 Oct 2026 and verified against YTD totals.
  • September 2026 figures are Preliminary to Quarter Close. September, Q3 and YTD results will be updated when the final quarter-end statements are issued.
  • August 2026 reflects the prior-month column of the September statements, which trued up Kyle’s cost of sales ($1,806 favorable to its final August statement); a further $46 pre-August adjustment is reflected in Kyle’s YTD figures.
  • The final August statements booked cost-of-sales true-ups at all five shops (reducing August gross profit, EBITDA and net income by ≈$2–15K per shop) and a one-time credit of $90,656 in Renew Support’s Other (Inc)/Exp reflecting a change in the treatment of philanthropy — the credit affects net income only and has no effect on any EBITDA figure.
  • Company totals are computed by summing location columns plus the Support entity, and include all shops. Pre-calculated total columns in the source exports are not used. Q1 and Q3 are computed by summing the monthly statements; Q2 is from the quarterly date-range exports.
  • Renew — Plainview opened in April 2026 ($1.2K of sales in its launch month); its margins reflect startup-phase volume. It appears in carrier views only from April.
  • Corporate support (Core Support / Renew Support) is shown at company level in the profit bridge and headline net income; it is not allocated to individual shops.
  • May and June 2026 reflect statements re-exported 22 Oct 2026, which trim depreciation and true up cost of sales (June EBITDA: Kyle +$46, Amarillo +$700); the Amarillo item was booked after the September statements, so preliminary YTD figures differ from the sum of months by $700 until quarter close. Each month otherwise reflects the most recent statement issued: June per the July statements and July per the August statements, whose prior-month columns restate depreciation and accrual items booked after close (sales, gross profit and shop overhead are unchanged). May 2026 is as reported in the June statements; Q2 figures are from the 16 Jul date-range exports and predate later revisions. All such differences sit below shop gross profit and are small.
  • Insurance & referral mix, estimate volume and severity come from the Sales Analysis by Location, by Insurance reports (monthly for January–September 2026, plus Q1 and Q2 quarterly views). The "prior" comparison in those reports is the same period one year earlier. Pre-tax estimate revenue can differ from GL sales in the income statements due to write-offs, discounts, changes to prior-period sales, and timing (generally under 1%).
  • USAA and USAA Casualty Insurance Company are combined as "USAA" in the carrier mix. "Customer pay" is retail work not billed to a carrier.
