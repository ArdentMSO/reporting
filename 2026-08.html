  • Each month reflects the most recent statement issued: June per the July statements and July per the August statements, whose prior-month columns restate depreciation and accrual items booked after close (sales, gross profit and shop overhead are unchanged). May 2026 is as reported in the June statements; Q2 figures are from the 16 Jul date-range exports and predate later revisions. All such differences sit below shop gross profit and are small.
  • Insurance & referral mix, estimate volume and severity come from the Sales Analysis by Location, by Insurance reports (Q1: 1/1–3/31/2026; Q2: 4/1–6/30/2026; July: 7/1–7/31/2026). The "prior" comparison in those reports is the same period one year earlier. Pre-tax estimate revenue differs slightly from GL sales in the income statements due to timing and system differences (generally under 1%; e.g., Amarillo July differs by about 0.8%).
  • USAA and USAA Casualty Insurance Company are combined as "USAA" in the carrier mix. "Customer pay" is retail work not billed to a carrier.
